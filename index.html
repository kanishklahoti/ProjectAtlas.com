P.U.L.S.E. FRAMEWORK

Five pillars.
One intelligent urban network.

BIOFLOW does not treat roads, signals and environment as separate systems. Its pillars create a coordinated loop of sensing, decision and adaptation.

P

Photosynthetic & Environmental Infrastructure

Environmental intelligence stations, AQI sensing and testable microalgae infrastructure concepts.

U

Urban Bio-Inspired Intelligence

Physarum, ant-colony and emissions-aware computation for adaptive network routes.

L

Living & Adaptive Road Infrastructure

Dynamic capacity, reversible lanes and future connected-vehicle reservations.

S

Smart & Synchronised Coordination

Adaptive signals, junction communication and predictive digital twin testing.

E

Ecological & Emergency Mobility

Pollution-aware choices and safe emergency movement through coordinated corridors.
